# TradeBooks Data Processing Terms (DPA)

**Draft — last updated 24 August 2026**

These terms apply where a TradeBooks customer is controller and TradeBooks processes personal data on that customer's behalf as processor.

## Processing details
- Subject matter: provision of TradeBooks bookkeeping software and related support.
- Duration: for the customer relationship plus the documented deletion/retention period.
- Nature/purpose: hosting, organising, importing, classifying, reconciling, securing, backing up and presenting bookkeeping/customer data as instructed through use of the service.
- Data types may include identity/contact data, business records, payment-account/transaction information, invoice/receipt information, correspondence and technical/security data.
- Data subjects may include the customer's staff, contractors, suppliers, customers and other persons represented in bookkeeping records.

## Processor commitments
TradeBooks will, where Article 28 UK GDPR applies: process personal data only on documented instructions unless required by law; ensure authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality; implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures; impose appropriate data-protection terms on subprocessors; assist the controller, taking account of the nature of processing, with data-subject rights and applicable security/breach/DPIA obligations; delete or return personal data at the end of services subject to lawful retention; and make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance available to the controller and support appropriate audits/inspections subject to reasonable safeguards.

## Subprocessors
Customer gives general authorisation for subprocessors necessary to provide the service, subject to TradeBooks maintaining a current subprocessor list and a process for material changes. **[COMPLETE PRODUCTION SUBPROCESSOR REGISTER].**

## International transfers
TradeBooks will ensure a lawful transfer mechanism is used where required for restricted international transfers. **[DOCUMENT EACH PRODUCTION TRANSFER].**

## Security schedule
At minimum: least-privilege access; secure authentication; encrypted transport; managed secrets; production access controls; logging/monitoring; vulnerability/dependency management; backups and recovery appropriate to the service; incident response; and secure deletion/retention procedures.

## Controller responsibilities
The customer is responsible for its instructions, lawful basis, transparency to its data subjects, accuracy of instructions and ensuring it has authority to provide personal data to TradeBooks.

## Priority
If these terms conflict with the main service agreement regarding processor obligations, these Data Processing Terms prevail for the affected processing.
