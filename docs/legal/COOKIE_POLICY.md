# TradeBooks Cookie Policy

**Draft for UK launch — last updated 24 August 2026**

TradeBooks may use cookies or similar storage technologies.

## Strictly necessary technologies
These may be required for sign-in, session management, security, fraud prevention, load balancing and remembering essential service state. Where the law permits, these operate without optional-cookie consent because the service cannot function properly without them.

## Optional analytics or advertising
TradeBooks must not set non-essential analytics, advertising or similar cookies/technologies until the user has been given clear information and the legally required choice/consent. Rejecting optional cookies must not prevent access to core functionality.

## Production cookie register
Before launch, complete and maintain the following for every cookie/storage item: name; provider; purpose; category; first/third party; duration; data recipients; and whether consent is required.

**Current rule:** do not add optional analytics/advertising until a compliant consent mechanism and register are in place.

## Managing choices
Where optional technologies are enabled, users must be able to change or withdraw their choice as easily as they gave it, via a persistent cookie/privacy settings control.
