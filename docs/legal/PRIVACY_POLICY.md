## 2. Data we process
Depending on the features used, we may process account/profile information; business details; bank account and transaction information obtained through an authorised Open Banking provider after the required authorisation/consent; bookkeeping records and transaction classifications; invoices, receipts and uploaded documents; support communications; security/audit information; and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not need or ask users to give us online-banking passwords or PINs.

## 3. Why we process data and lawful bases
We process data to provide and administer TradeBooks, authenticate users, import authorised banking data, categorise and reconcile transactions, produce bookkeeping records and reports, provide support, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with law and improve the reliability of the service.

Depending on the activity, our lawful basis will normally be performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or consent where the law requires consent. Consent for access to payment-account information is handled in accordance with the applicable Open Banking flow and provider requirements and is separate from general marketing consent.

## 4. Open Banking
TradeBooks integrates with an Open Banking provider such as TrueLayer. Bank connection is initiated by the user and the relevant bank/provider authentication and consent screens govern access to payment-account information. TradeBooks must not imply that a general acceptance of these terms itself constitutes the regulatory consent required for account-information access.

The exact regulatory model for production (for example, TradeBooks acting under an authorised provider's permitted model, or TradeBooks obtaining its own registration/authorisation if required) must be confirmed before production bank-data access is offered.

## 7. Retention
We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described, contractual requirements, security and applicable legal/accounting obligations. A production retention schedule must define periods by data category. ## 8. Security
We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the risk, including access controls, secure authentication, encrypted transport, secret-management practices, logging and restricted production access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to access their personal data, correct inaccurate information, request erasure, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data in applicable circumstances, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these outputs are suggestions and may be wrong. Users remain responsible for reviewing records and obtaining qualified accounting or tax advice where appropriate. We will disclose material automated decision-making if any feature reaches the legal threshold requiring that disclosure.

## 11. Cookies and similar technologies
Strictly necessary technologies may be used to provide authentication, security and core functionality. Any non-essential analytics/advertising technologies must not be activated until the required user choice/consent has been obtained. See the Cookie Policy.
